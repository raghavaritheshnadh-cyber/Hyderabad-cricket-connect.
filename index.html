Safety first

Play fair. Play safe. Go home safe.

Safety is part of every HCC match journey. Verify the opponent and venue, agree on match details and never feel pressured to attend.

  • Players under 18 must have parent or guardian permission.
  • Inform your family about the venue, date and time.
  • Use suitable protective equipment, especially for leather-ball cricket.
  • Do not share personal details publicly.
  • Travel with trusted teammates or an adult.
  • Stop and seek help if anything feels unsafe.

Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a team to register?

No. Individual players will be able to register independently and look for teams.

Do I need to register again for every match?

The plan is one account and one profile. You can use that profile for different HCC services and match requests.

Can I book a ground instantly?

Not yet. Ground enquiries will need confirmation from the ground owner before a booking is confirmed.

Is HCC fully operational now?

No. This is the first public information preview. Secure registration, matching, booking enquiries and dashboards are still being developed.
