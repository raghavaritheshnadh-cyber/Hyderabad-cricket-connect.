Hyderabad Cricket Connect

Find your team. Find your match.

Let's arrange a match!

Connect with cricket players and teams across Hyderabad.

🏏 **Play safe. Follow the rules.**
Choose your cricket format and follow the ground's safety instructions. Leather-ball matches require appropriate protective equipment.

Your number should only be shared with the other team after both sides agree. This demo does not send or store your number online.

How it works

1. Enter your match requirements.

2. Find a suitable team in your area.

3. Send a match request.

4. Both captains agree on the details before confirming.

Team search, online registration, shared match requests, and AI calling are planned features. They are not connected in this prototype yet.
