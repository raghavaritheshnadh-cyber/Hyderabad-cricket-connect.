How it works

1. Install CricHeroes and create an account.

2. Enter your match requirements.

3. Find a suitable team in your area.

4. Both captains agree on match details before confirming.

This is an early prototype. Online registration, shared match requests, team search and automatic CricHeroes account verification are not connected yet.
